Yuki Desktop includes noVNC 1.7.0.

Upstream source: https://github.com/novnc/noVNC/tree/v1.7.0
The original source is distributed in the pinned @novnc/novnc package.
Yuki Desktop bundles its library through Vite with the local source patch
patches/@novnc+novnc+1.7.0.patch, which exposes the negotiated UTF-8 clipboard
capability through the existing capabilities event. The patch is included in
the Yuki Desktop source repository:
https://github.com/Institute-of-Agent-Life/Yuki-Desktop
